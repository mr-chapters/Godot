# 🔊 SFX Makers' Book — Make Your Game Sound Alive

**Who this book is for:** sound-effect makers, music creators, and developers who want game audio to communicate clearly. You can create sounds yourself or use properly licensed assets.

## Table of contents

1. [Sound Design from Zero](01-sound-design-from-zero.md) — sound envelopes, emotion, sound palettes, tools, and repeated listening.
2. [Bring Sound into Godot](02-import-and-play-audio.md) — import audio, choose audio nodes, trigger playback, and troubleshoot silence.
3. [Music, Loops, and a Clean Mix](03-music-loops-and-audio-mixing.md) — loop points, buses, volume balance, and audio managers.
4. [Licences, Voice, and a Final Audio Pack](04-licenses-voice-and-final-audio-pack.md) — attribution records, folder layout, voice consent, and quality control.

Always check the current licence of the specific asset and include required attribution. Do not assume all files on one platform share the same terms.

## Audio production checklist
- [ ] Files have descriptive names.
- [ ] Short effects do not become tiring when repeated.
- [ ] Music loops have no unwanted clicks or gaps.
- [ ] Music and SFX can be adjusted independently where useful.
- [ ] Important gameplay cues remain audible.
- [ ] Positional sounds come from the correct world object.
- [ ] Sources and licence terms are recorded.
- [ ] The complete mix is tested in the running game.

**Graduation challenge:** deliver a complete sound pack for a small game, with UI click, jump, pickup, damage, victory, ambience, and music. Include a README with source and licence information.
